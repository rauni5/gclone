# Code of Conduct

gclone is a project for people who want to get unfamiliar code running without friction.
We want the community around it to feel the same way: welcoming, patient and practical.

This code of conduct is adapted from the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the community:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people who made a mistake.
- Giving specific, constructive feedback, and accepting it gracefully.
- Assuming good intent, and asking when something is unclear.
- Crediting other people's work and ideas.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in any form.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about a person's identity, background, skill level
  or country.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Threats, intimidation, or deliberately provoking others.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as their address or email, without
  permission.
- Posting secrets, credentials or private data in issues, even your own.
- Spam, or using the project's spaces mainly to advertise something unrelated.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when
someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit,
hide or remove comments, close or lock issues, and remove or block people whose behaviour
they consider inappropriate, and will explain why when it is appropriate to do so.

To report a problem, email the maintainer at the address on their GitHub profile
(<https://github.com/rauni5>). Reports are handled privately and in good faith, and the
reporter's privacy is respected.

Maintainers will consider what happened and respond proportionately: a private
clarification, a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban for serious or
repeated behaviour.
